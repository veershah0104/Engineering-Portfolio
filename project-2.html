Formula Student Car Design and Assembly

El Pajaro Grande: a Formula Student China car designed to the rules and validated with FEA.

RoleCAD Engineer
ToolsSolidWorks, FEA
TeamTeam of 3

The problem

Design a car for the Formula Student China competition that complies with all of its rules and regulations.

My process

1. Design

Final suspension fitted to the hardpoints, with push rods at the front and rear, rockers, and spring and damper units.

2. Analyse

Static FEA on the chassis and suspension: max stress 184.2 MPa against a 220.6 MPa yield strength.

Subsystems

Front suspension

3° negative static camber, 0.5° toe-out, 18° KPI, 5° positive caster and a 7.5 mm scrub radius.

Drivetrain

A 4:1 gear ratio with a chain drive instead of a belt, for stable power to the wheel, less slippage and low weight.

Front wing

Built around a NACA 4412 airfoil.

Tools and software

SolidWorksSolidWorks Simulation (FEA)Manufacturing report

What failed

  • Two suspension designs failed the FEA load test, so I had to redesign the suspension system each time.

What worked

The final design complied with all the rules and passed every FEA strength test. I also made sure it could be manufactured, and wrote a manufacturing report that tells the factory how each part needs to be made.

Results
