Who I am

I've studied and lived in Hong Kong all my life, and I'm now studying Mechanical Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (class of 2028). I started as a home-grown engineer, fixing my mom's grinder and the small plugs and sockets around the house.

At 13 I began learning CAD modelling in school, which opened the door to ideas I couldn't build before. My goal since then has stayed the same: work on as many projects as I can, gather the experience, and eventually start a startup of my own.

I'm not perfect, and neither are my builds. A custom hinge I made for a table snapped three times before the fourth design finally held.
